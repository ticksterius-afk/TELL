# Research notes (from the two completed briefs)

## Education brief – key facts to cite
- Superpower Glass (Stanford/Voss 2019 JAMA Pediatrics, n=71): +4.58 Vineland socialization, gains faded 6 weeks after. Licensed to Cognoa; never clearly shipped.
- Brain Power / Empowered Brain (Affectiva SDK on Glass): ~$165k Indiegogo, 60 units.
- FaceSay RCT n=49 (Hopkins 2011): improved recognition and social interaction.
- Emotion Mirror (UCSD, Bartlett CERT): mimic-it/it-mimics-you game; production training with automated feedback improved recognition in adults, esp. engaged players. Never shipped.
- Ekman METT/SETT: $119/3mo, $229/6mo, $299/yr. Humintell MiX $29-89. App Store micro-expression apps: static photo quizzes, <10 ratings each. Nobody uses the camera.
- Yoodli: webcam eye-contact scoring least trusted; Toastmasters data backlash. Orai ~$12/mo. Poised acquired by Deepgram 2024.
- Luvly face yoga: Trustpilot 4.1 (3,845), subscription-trap complaints; face-yoga apps ~$370-420k/month avg (unverified, screensdesign). Alam 2018 JAMA Derm n=16.
- Facial palsy: Würzburg 2026 JMIR Rehab pilot, iPhone TrueDepth on-device, "Digital Facial Index", biofeedback exercises, n=21, SUS 88.3, patients preferred it. ImproveFP, FaceRehab.
- No camera-based pronunciation mouth-shape product at scale.
- FaceDance Challenge (DiffCat 2017, Visage SDK): mimic emojis to the beat, viral in a week, >10M downloads.
- Evidence: Hurley 2012 (n=306; gains held 3-6 weeks, best w/ practice+feedback); Matsumoto & Hwang 2011. Jordan 2019: METT doesn't transfer to lie detection. Zhang 2021 meta-analysis (n=595): autism emotion-recognition training gains robust, weak generalisation. Imitation improves recognition (n=55). Barrett 2019. Double-empathy/masking critique.
- Duolingo: 47.7M DAU (Q2 2025), >10M 1-year streaks, 1/3 DAU have Friend Streak, churn 47%→28%; streak at top +3% DAU; leagues +17% learning time. Streak screens falling 14%→8% of apps (only works when loop is genuine).
- White space: (1) camera-based PRODUCTION training with FACS feedback; (2) daily shared face puzzle w/ share card + friend streaks; (3) FACS self-study companion (FACS cert = weeks + $1,700); (4) actor/communicator expressivity; (5) palsy/post-stroke symmetry exercises; (6) privacy as product.
- Regulation: EU AI Act Art 5(1)(f) bans emotion inference in workplace/education institutions. Recital 18: mere detection of readily apparent expressions (smile/frown) and physical states (pain, fatigue) not "emotion recognition" unless used to infer emotions. Apple 5.1.2(vi) no face data for ads/marketing; 2.5.14 consent; 1.4.1 health claims; Kids Category. COPPA amended 2025: facial templates = personal info.

## Deception brief – key facts to cite
- Bond & DePaulo 2006: 54% accuracy (206 studies, 24,483 judges); audio better than video.
- DePaulo 2003: 158 cues, median d≈.10. Survivors: vocal pitch d≈.21, pupil dilation ~.39, pressed lips, chin raise, fewer illustrators. Gaze aversion d≈.03 (myth, though 75-country belief). Liars move LESS.
- Vrij, Hartwig & Granhag 2019: nonverbal cues "faint and unreliable".
- Porter & ten Brinke 2008: falsified expressions more inconsistent; masked → higher blink rate, neutralized → lower; happiness easiest to fake, fear/disgust hardest; only ~2% of 700 expressions had a micro-expression. 2012 "Cry me a river": micro-expressions 6 deceivers vs 8 genuine (non-diagnostic); what separated deceptive pleaders: full AU1+2 (failed sadness) + subtle AU12 leak; multi-cue model 90%.
- Jordan 2019: METT training → below chance. Wizards Project: 50/20,000 explainable by chance (Bond & Uysal 2007).
- Leal & Vrij 2008 (n=26): blink rate DROPS during the lie, rebounds after; Perelman 2014 replicated suppression (81% in-sample) not the rebound. = cognitive load signal, within-person.
- Ekman/Hager/Friesen 1981: deliberate expressions more asymmetric. Krumhuber & Manstead 2009: 83% can fake Duchenne; Gunnery: 24% of fakes were Duchenne.
- Barrett 2019 undermines face→emotion→lie twice.
- EyeDetect (as low as 50% for guilty), iBorderCtrl/Silent Talker flagged honest reporter, AVATAR no independent validation, TSA SPOT (GAO-14-159). von Schenk 2024: AI text 67% vs humans 47%, but people accuse far more. Markowitz MSU Nov 2025: genAI lie-biased.
- Ranking: (1) blink suppression vs baseline; (2) AU-level inconsistency/failed simulation; (3) asymmetry; (4) Duchenne (weak); (5) micro-expressions (rare); (6) gaze/face-touch (myth).
- Market: "LIE DETECTOR FREE" 1M+ iOS, 2.2★/155K; "Truth and Lie Detector" 1.5M, 4.4★; "Face Lie Detector" tap-left/right prank 138K 3.5★. Truthsayer open source (MediaPipe + FER + pulse). LiarLiar.ai Zoom overlay. "Imposter – Party Word Game" 6M+, 4.8★/52K. The Traitors 40,000 applicants S2 vs 1,500 S1. Hasbro Lie Detector $35 (voice). TikTok #liedetectortest billions of views; "hand over face lie detector" trend ~1M plays per video.
- Policy: Apple 1.1.6 bans trick/joke functionality ("for entertainment" doesn't cure it); 1.4.1. Google Play "misleading claims" even if joke. EU AI Act: emotion recognition high-risk by default (Annex III 1(c)) unless 6(3) carve-out; Art 50(3) transparency; polygraph-like = high-risk only in law enforcement/migration. Lever: output movement metrics not truth/emotion labels.
- Angles: reframe to poker-face/self-control; within-person baseline; mechanics: Two Truths & a Lie w/ leak-meter replay, Stare-down blink duel, "Fake it" (pose sadness/disgust, score AU1+2/AU12 leakage), Interrogation hot-seat with shareable 3-s slow-mo leak clip w/ AU heatmap; the clip is the viral unit; "science mode" tooltip with real effect sizes.
